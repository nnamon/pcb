Independent reviewer, 14 Nov 2026, the working tree on branch `pcb-0.8.0` before the commit. The reviewer was given the files and the claims of the CHANGELOG, `DESIGN.md` and `README.md`, not the reasoning. The report is as written; the closures follow it.

### Findings, most serious first

1. **SHOULD FIX: `[[edge]]` reports a part inside a rounded board as outside when it is level with an arc's end. Confirmed by running.**
   - Where: `src/check.rs:187` (`edge_ahead`, the half-open test `(b0 <= pb) != (b1 <= pb)`) with `src/board.rs:337` (`arc_points` recomputes the arc's ends by trigonometry, so they differ from the line ends in the last bits).
   - Input: outline at (0, 0), 40 x 30 mm, four lines and four `gr_arc` corners of radius 2 mm. Part A at (2, 15) bound `top`, part B at (20, 2) bound `right`, `max_mm` 16 and 21.
   - Output: `A is outside the board's outline` and `B is outside the board's outline`. A part at (2.001, 15) passes. The same outline at (100, 100) passes.
   - Why: a part on the grid line of a corner radius is ordinary, and the finding fails a correct board. A port of the two functions (`arcport.py`) predicts 12 wrong of 480 tangent positions over six origins and ten radii; two were run in the binary and both failed.
   - Fix: snap arc ends to the stored `start` and `end`, or count crossings with a tolerance.

2. **SHOULD FIX: "more attempts can only match or better it" (`DESIGN.md:443`) is false when the better attempts fail the gate. Confirmed by running.**
   - Where: `src/route.rs:1298` and `:1321`. The runs are sorted by open nets and only the first two go to the gate.
   - Input: `synth_board.py base`, region `10,5,50,35`, stand-in java. Attempt 0 lays nothing; attempts 1 and 2 each close N with a track that crosses L.
   - Output: `--attempts 1` gives `done`, `out.kicad_pcb` written, `still_open ['N', 'S']`. `--attempts 3` gives `rejected`, `out.rejected.kicad_pcb`, 1 regression, `attempt_used 1`. The clean first attempt is never tried.
   - Fix: walk every attempt in order at the gate, or drop the sentence.

3. **SHOULD FIX: later attempts repeat an earlier order, so "each attempt after the first gives the router the nets in another order" does not hold. Confirmed by running.**
   - Where: `src/route.rs:413`; `README.md:275`, `CHANGELOG.md:16`, `DESIGN.md:350`, the `--attempts` help in `src/main.rs`.
   - Input and output: 14 nets, `--attempts 6` gives `net_turn [0, 7, 2, 7, 4, 7]`; `route_region4.dsn` is byte-identical to `route_region2.dsn`. 21 nets, `--attempts 5` gives `[0, 7, 14, 3, 7]`. 6 nets, `--attempts 8` gives `[0, 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 0, 1]`; the seventh file is the first. 2 nets give `[0, 1, 0, 1]`, 1 net `[0, 0, 0]`.
   - Why: README advises `--attempts 4`. On a board whose DSN has 14 nets the fourth attempt is the second again, and Freerouting repeats itself on the same file by the benchmark's own account. Any net count that is a multiple of 7 repeats from attempt n/7 + 1.
   - Fix: pick a turn not yet used, and stop when every turn has been tried.

4. **SHOULD FIX: an outline with a `gr_curve` is read with a gap, and `[[edge]]` reports a part on the board as outside. Confirmed by running.**
   - Where: `src/board.rs:425` reads `gr_line`, `gr_rect`, `gr_arc`, `gr_poly`, `gr_circle` only.
   - Input: 40 x 30 mm outline whose bottom edge is a bezier from (40, 30) through (30, 20), (10, 20) to (0, 30). Part at (20, 10) bound `bottom`.
   - Output: `A is 10.00 mm outside the bottom edge`. The part is inside, 12.5 mm from the curve. `min_web_mm` cannot see the curve either.
   - README's known limits name outlines held in a footprint, not curves.

5. **SHOULD FIX: a rounded `gr_rect` (KiCad 10 writes `(radius 8)`) is read as a sharp rectangle. Confirmed by running.**
   - Where: `src/board.rs:430`.
   - Input: `gr_rect` 40 x 30 mm with `SetCornerRadius(8 mm)`, part at (1, 1) bound `top`.
   - Output: `A is 1.00 mm from the top edge`. The part is off the board, in the cut corner. The claim "a part outside the outline is a finding" misses it, and distances near a corner are measured to an edge that is not there.

6. **SHOULD FIX: a part on a right-hand or lower edge, bound to a perpendicular side, is reported outside; on a left or upper edge it is measured. Confirmed by running.**
   - Where: `src/check.rs:187`, the half-open rule.
   - Input: 40 x 30 mm rectangle, `max_mm` 0.001.
   - Output: (0, 15) `top` gives `15.00 mm from the top edge`; (40, 15) `top` gives `outside the board's outline`. (20, 0) `left` gives 20.00; (20, 30) `left` gives outside. The corner (40, 30) is outside for all four sides, including `right` and `bottom`, where (0, 0) gives 0. On the notch's wall, (18, 5) `top` gives `5.00 mm outside the top edge`.
   - Why: an edge connector's origin often sits on the edge. The unit test covers only `right` on the right edge.

7. **SHOULD FIX: `README.md:195` still says `edge` measures to "its edge of the outline's bounding box". Confirmed by reading.**
   - The check table contradicts README's own known limits (line 251), `DESIGN.md:257` and the code. `src/project.rs:419` and the `min_web_mm` field comment at `:430` say the same old thing, and README sends the reader to those structs for field meanings.

8. **SHOULD FIX: the benchmark supports less than three sentences drawn from it. Read only.**
   - "The longer the stub the worse" (`docs/bench/route-0.8.0.md:36`, `DESIGN.md:445`): the rows are 0.15 mm 2, 5, 1 (and 2); 0.3 mm 14, 7, 6; 0.5 mm 5, 6; 0.4 and 0.9 mm alternating 4, 6. Both 0.5 mm runs beat all three 0.3 mm runs. The figures support "0.3 mm and longer were worse than 0.15 mm".
   - "Freerouting 2.5.0 repeats itself on the same file, so N attempts were one attempt N times" (`CHANGELOG.md:16`, `route.rs:400` comment): the same file says "With any track on the board before routing, Freerouting no longer repeats itself". The claim holds for a stripped board. A board with a prewire or kept copper is the usual `pcb route` input, and there the old attempts did differ.
   - The evidence for repeating is "the same three nets" in two `--attempts 4` runs and the three 0.6.1 runs. Same nets open is not shown to be the same copper.
   - The eight-of-eleven count, the six-order row and the `--attempts 6` result match the tables.

9. **MINOR: an unclosed outline gives an `edge` finding with a wrong reason. Confirmed by running.**
   - Input: rectangle with the top line missing, part at (20, 15) bound `top`. Output: `U1 is 15.00 mm outside the top edge`; 15 mm is the distance to the bottom edge behind it. With a 0.01 mm gap at a corner, a part in the gap's column gets the same message. The other three sides measure correctly.

10. **MINOR: `paste-unassembled` misses paste drawn as `fp_curve` or `fp_text`. Confirmed by running.**
    - Where: `src/check.rs:420`. Input: R2 left out of the CPL, pads without paste, an `fp_curve` (or `fp_text`) on `F.Paste`; pcbnew loads both on F.Paste. Output: no `paste-unassembled` finding. `fp_poly`, `fp_rect`, `fp_circle`, `fp_line` and `fp_arc` are found.

11. **MINOR: `silk-owner` ignores vertical justification and "keep upright" off, and DESIGN and README don't say so. Confirmed by running.**
    - Where: `src/check.rs:208`. A 1 mm reference with `justify top` or `bottom`: the middle is 0.61 mm from pcbnew's box centre (8 of 8 cases). With keep upright off and a drawn angle of 180 or 200 degrees it is 4.08 mm off, the text taken to run the other way (4 of 4; the 4 drawn at 0 or 20 degrees are right). The code comment admits the second; `DESIGN.md` says "KiCad draws it upright" without the exception.

12. **MINOR: a small pad under a designator can fall between the sample rows. Confirmed by running.**
    - Where: `src/check.rs:218`, rows at 0 and ±0.4 of the height. Input: `SW1000` at 1 mm, its own pad 0.6 mm below the middle line; X1's 0.3 x 0.25 mm pad under the text 0.2 mm below the middle line. Output: no finding. The same with a pad 0.5 mm tall gives `SW2000's designator lies over X2's pad and 0.20 mm from its own part`. Pads under 0.4 of the text height in one direction can be missed.

13. **MINOR: README's pin example mixes versions.** `README.md:114` now reads `pcb = "0.8"` and, in the same sentence, "a pin of `"0.7.0"` accepts that version alone". Read only.

### Checked and found correct

- **KiCad's stored flip.** A pad at local (2, 1), footprint turned 0, 30, 90, 135, 200, 270, −45 degrees, flipped left to right and top to bottom (14 flipped, 7 not): the file has `(at 2 -1 …)` in all 14. Left to right stores 180 − angle, top to bottom stores −angle.
- **Bottom CPL rotation and origin.** One release of paddle-keyer v1 with every assembled part turned to one of 9 angles and flipped neither way, left to right or top to bottom (U1 and J1 kept on top; their `[[paste]]` rules are refused on the bottom). Every LCSC code had one of five offsets with a y part and a `rotation_offset_deg` of 0, 90, 180, 270 or 30. Probe pads at JLCPCB's origin and 1 mm along its x and y axes went on before pcbnew's flip. Mid X, Mid Y, Rotation and Layer matched for 48 of 48 within 0.002 mm and 0.01 degrees, and the footprint is unmirrored seen from the bottom. The oracle uses the convention the claim states (bottom view turned over left to right, anticlockwise positive), so it proves the arithmetic, not JLCPCB's reading. `cpl-bottom` and `bottom-cpl-unverified` both appeared with the new wording.
- **The outside source.** `fabrication.py` (fetched 14 Nov 2026) has `rotation = (180 - rotation) % 360` for a bottom footprint, then adds the correction. Its position offset maps to pcb's `cpl_offset_mm` with the same sign change on both sides, and it writes positions unmirrored. The comment is fair for the rotation.
- **`pcb parts` on bottom parts.** All 48 assembled parts turned by 0, 30, 90, 135, 200, 270 degrees and flipped each way: 576 solves, all equal to the top's, 0 findings, J1 `[0, 0.125]` and J2 `[-0.1, 0]` kept. Q1, U3, J1, J2 and K1 with their pads turned a quarter, half or three quarters inside the footprint (top solves to 90, 270, 90, 180, 270 with the offsets turned): 40 bottom solves equal the top's. A wrong y sign on a bottom J1 is a `J1 cpl-offset` finding.
- **`[[edge]]` distances.** L-shaped `gr_poly`, slot notch, diamond with rays through vertices, round board, circle and rectangle cut-outs, parts inside cut-outs, in the notch, beyond an edge, level with corners, reversed `gr_rect`: right apart from findings 1, 4, 5, 6 and 9. A circle is a 64-gon, so 1.01 for 1.00 mm at radius 5 mm. Rounded rectangle at (100, 100), 34 cases, all right.
- **`min_web_mm`.** Holes beside a circular cut-out (1.00, 0.60, 2.50 mm), a round board's rim (1.50 mm) and a notch wall the part is not bound to (1.00 mm): all right.
- **`silk-owner` placement.** 162 designators (footprint at 0, 90, 30, 200, 270, 135; text at 0, 90, 45; left, centre, right; front, flipped left to right, flipped top to bottom) are within 0.078 mm of pcbnew's box centre. Another 12 at text angles 180, 270, 120 and 4 with the mirror flag set against the side are within 0.3 mm.
- **Text width.** One character width per letter is within 0.25 mm for R1, R111, C10, U1, SW100, J11, L1, L111, M1, W88, Q100, TP1, D12, FB10, X1; `MW8M` is 0.48 mm wider and `I1I1` 1.04 mm narrower. Neighbour pads placed 0.25 to 0.3 mm past the true ink end gave no false finding (3 cases).
- **Dense layouts.** 52 0402 parts with 0.6 mm designators beside or above them: columns at 1.0 and 1.2 mm pitch, rows at 1.0 and 2.2 mm, two facing rows, two groups on the back. No finding. Six misplaced by my script, lying across neighbours, gave five true findings.
- **Designator over its own pad and another's.** No finding, as the rule states.
- **`paste-unassembled`.** Item `R2 drawn` with the `pcb sync` fix for the five shape kinds; count 2 for two shapes; B.Paste only with `bottom` in `machine_sides`; F.Paste ignored with bottom only; silent with no assembled part and on an assembled part; found on a `hand_fit` part and a `dnp` part; pads on `*.Paste` found for top and for bottom only.
- **`nets_turned` on real DSNs.** Names `"A B"`, `"N (1)"`, `"x)y"`, `"(z"`, `"/sheet/SIG~{CS}"`, and `Q"1` (KiCad writes `Q''1`): each later file is the first list turned by `net_turn`, entries whole, class lines and the rest byte-identical. A 28-net board gives `[0, 7, 14, 21]`. One net leaves the file alone. KiCad's net order is the same over four exports of one board.
- **Attempt handling.** Stops at the first attempt with nothing open (two DSNs written for `--attempts 3`); a tie keeps the first; the run without keep-outs reports `net_turn 0`. `schemas/route.json` accepted 8 route outputs, including five attempts and a named-nets run.
- **Contract.** Check outputs with `R2 drawn`, the new edge and silk messages, a bottom `pcb parts` and a bottom release pass `contract_check.py`. `docs/codes.md` has the reworded `bottom-cpl-unverified`; no new code was added. `Cargo.toml` and `Cargo.lock` say 0.8.0, and `pcb --version` prints `pcb 0.8.0`.
