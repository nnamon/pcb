# Review 21: the M5a checks (pcb 0.5.0)

Independent reviewer, 13 Nov 2026, worktree at commit 60a8a09 plus the uncommitted `pcb new` work. The reviewer was given the files and the CHANGELOG's claims, not the reasoning. The report is as written; the closures follow it.

## Report

Review of `pcb` 0.5.0 (M5a checks), worktree /home/claude/wt-pcb, binary `target/release/pcb` (0.5.0). Nothing under the worktree was written. All probes are in /tmp/review_m5a (8 MB): `lib/mk.py` is the board builder, `p1.py` to `p12.py` are the probes, and each board folder holds its `pcb.toml` and work files. KiCad 10.0.8 and Freerouting 2.5.0 were on the host, so most findings were run. Line numbers are from the files as I read them; `src/main.rs` and `src/project.rs` were changed by someone else in the worktree during the review, so those may have shifted.

### Findings, most serious first

**1. The gate's art-free copy hides a mask bridge that `pcb edit` makes. Confirmed by running (`p8.py`).**
- Where: `src/gate.rs:122` (`without_art`), `src/art.rs:217` (`without_surface_art`).
- Board: a `[[art]]` mask opening on F.Mask from (12, 8) to (18, 12); pads of net A at y = 9.5 and net B at y = 10.5 on either side of it. `pcb art` passes.
- Input: `pcb edit` with two `add_track` operations, A and B, both crossing the opening.
- Output: `edit: done`, `regressions: []`, `DRC 2 -> 0`. kicad-cli on the written board reports `solder_mask_bridge` (error) 1; on the input it reports none.
- The same applies to `route`, `zone`, `place` and `paste`: any copper they put under mask artwork, or silkscreen they move onto artwork, is not judged at the gate. `pcb check` still sees it, because it copies the whole board.
- The CHANGELOG reasons that "only `pcb art` changes it". That holds for the artwork but not for violations between artwork and new copper.

**2. Two unplated (or no-net) holes too close pass plain `pcb check`. Confirmed by running (`p7.py` A, `p12.py`).**
- Where: `src/fab.rs:82-83`.
- The rule `A.Net == B.Net` gives 0.254 mm. Two holes with no net both have net "", so they match the same-net rule and are never held to `hole_hole_mm` 0.5.
- Board: two NPTH pads of 1.0 mm drill, 0.3 mm edge to edge. Plain `pcb check` has no `hole_to_hole` finding. `pcb check --fab` reports `dfm hole_hole drill: 0.300 mm edge to edge ... (limit 0.5 mm)`.
- So the KiCad rule and the read-back disagree, though the CHANGELOG says they measure against the same numbers.
- Same-net vias and PTH pads 0.1 mm apart, and other-net vias 0.4 mm apart, are found by the KiCad rule.

**3. `critical-open` does not fire when the board's `.kicad_pro` ignores `unconnected_items`. Confirmed by running (`p9f`).**
- Where: `src/route.rs:697`.
- The check runs DRC on the board with its own severities. `check.rs` forces this severity to error on its copies; route does not.
- Board: `p9a` (critical net C unrouted) gives `refused critical-open`. The same board with `"unconnected_items": "ignore"` gives `done`, `critical_kept: ["C"]`, `still_open: []`, no warning. The router ran with C open.

**4. `[[edge]]` measures to the outline's bounding box, from the footprint's anchor. Confirmed by running (`p3.py` A, B, C).**
- Where: `src/check.rs:334-362`, `src/board.rs:594`.
- L-shaped outline (40 x 30 with the top-right 20 x 15 cut away):
  - H1, a 1.0 mm hole 0.2 mm from the notch's edge, bound `right` with `min_web_mm = 3`: no finding.
  - S1, 2 mm from that edge with `max_mm = 3`: `S1 is 22.00 mm from the right edge`. A false positive.
  - S2 at x = 45, off the 40 mm board, `max_mm = 2`: no finding. The distance is negative and only `d > max` is tested.
- A board with no top-level Edge.Cuts: `bbox()` returns (0, 0, 100, 100) without saying so. A part at x = 99 passes "within 2 mm of the right edge", and one at x = 50 is reported 50 mm from an edge that does not exist.
- The web is measured only to the named edge: a hole 0.3 mm from the left edge, bound `top`, passes.
- The CHANGELOG says "a named edge of the outline"; DESIGN.md and `project.rs` say bounding box.

**5. `edge-setting` reads only top-level `gr_arc` and `gr_circle`. Confirmed by running (`p3.py` D, `p3e.py`, `p3f.py`).**
- Where: `src/check.rs:308-309`.
- Missed: a rounded `gr_rect` (KiCad 10 writes `(radius 4)`) with a GND zone and the setting at 0.3 gives no finding. `--fab` on that board reports `dfm cu_edge F.Cu: copper 0.299 mm from the board edge`, the fault the check exists for.
- Missed: the same arcs held in a footprint (`fp_arc` on Edge.Cuts) give no finding.
- False positive: arcs plus only a keep-out rule area (no pour) are flagged. `b.zones` counts rule areas.
- By reading only: arcs inside a `gr_poly` are not seen, and a custom rule in `.kicad_dru` that sets the edge clearance is not read.

**6. Silkscreen over an unplated hole whose pad has no mask layers is counted as a tented via. Confirmed by running (`p7b.py`).**
- Where: `src/gerber_helper.py:635`.
- Board: a 2 mm NPTH pad with layers F.Cu and B.Cu only, a silkscreen line across it. Result: warning `silk-on-via ... (F.Silk: 1)`, no `silk_on_hole`.
- The same hole with the usual mask layers is found (`0.399 mm² of silkscreen over a hole the mask leaves open`).
- The test "mask opening touches the hole" does not tell a tented via from an open hole with no mask opening drawn. Plating or hole size would.

**7. `silk` text findings count owners, and all board text is one owner. Confirmed by running (`p4.py`).**
- Where: `src/check.rs:397-432`.
- A waiver for `text height 0.4 mm` with `max = 1` covers any number of board texts at that height.
- Not read at all:
  - text in a TrueType face (0.4 mm high, 0.05 mm stroke: no finding);
  - `gr_text_box` and `fp_text_box` (0.4 mm, 0.08 mm stroke: no finding);
  - by reading, dimensions and tables.
- The CHANGELOG says "KiCad's own silkscreen text (visible footprint fields, footprint and board text)" with no exception.

**8. Quality measures skip track arcs and short top segments. Confirmed by running (`p6.py` B).**
- Where: `src/quality.rs:152`, `src/quality.rs:233`.
- A power net laid only as a 0.15 mm track arc, with `power_width_mm = 0.5`: the net is not in `nets` and has no `width` finding. Only `b.tracks` is read, and a net with no segments is skipped.
- A top net running 20 mm directly over a bottom net, laid as 0.9 mm segments, with `max_broadside_mm = 2`: `broadside: []`. Top segments under 1.0 mm are dropped before the overlap is summed.
- By reading only: broadside looks at F.Cu over B.Cu alone, so inner layers are never compared.
- The `detour` message says "pads 20.0 mm apart" for a Manhattan tree length; the pads were 14.1 mm apart.

**9. `via-in-pad` uses the pad's bounding rectangle. Confirmed by running (`p5.py` A).**
- Where: `src/check.rs:128-131`, `src/check.rs:274-279`.
- Missed: a custom pad with a 0.4 mm anchor and a 2 x 2 mm polygon primitive, via at (0.7, 0.7) on the polygon. `Pad.prims` exists in `board.rs` but `pad_gap` ignores it.
- False positives, both small:
  - a round 1.0 mm pad with a via whose hole edge is 0.015 mm outside the circle;
  - a 2 x 1 oval with a via outside its rounded end.
- A via under a paste window is reported under the window's empty pad number, as item `W1.`, not under the copper pad it sits on.
- `src/route.rs:1255` has the same geometry copied, not shared.

**10. `[[near]]` takes the first pad with the number. Confirmed by running (`p6.py` A).**
- Where: `src/check.rs:320`.
- U1 has two pads numbered 1, at (10, 10) and (30, 10); C1 is 1.5 mm from the second. Result: `C1 is 21.50 mm from U1.1, over 2 mm`.
- This affects split thermal pads and repeated shield pins.

**11. `--max-cross-mm` may count a hand-laid track that stage 2 splits. By reading only.**
- Where: `src/main.rs:655-657`.
- "New" segments are those whose (ends, layer) key is not in the stage-1 board. `route.rs` rewrites a kept track as pieces when new copper joins its middle (`split_tracks`), so the pieces of an existing off-layer track would count as a new run and could reject a sound result.
- I could not show it: Freerouting left the net open on both boards I built (`p10`, `p10b`), and in plain `pcb route` too.
- Also by reading: the staged report is stage 2's alone. Stage 1's `vias_in_pads` warning and its added counts are dropped, and the longest-run figures are only in the human text, not in `data`.

**12. `paste-unassembled` reads pad layers only. Confirmed by running (`p5.py` B).**
- Where: `src/check.rs:263`.
- Paste drawn as an `fp_poly` on F.Paste in an unassembled footprint is not found.
- By reading only: a pad whose layers say `*.Paste` is not matched.

**13. `spec` is skipped when `pcb.toml` has no `[finish]`. By reading, plus `pcb init` output.**
- Where: `src/check.rs:302`.
- `pcb init` writes no `[finish]`, so a new project gets no spec judgement and no notice from `pcb check`. Only `release` warns (`finish-unset`).

**14. `npth_copper_mm` has no KiCad rule. By reading only.**
- Where: `src/fab.rs:75-100`.
- Copper to an unplated hole at 0.2 mm is judged only by the read-back (`--fab`, `release`). The CHANGELOG lists it among limits `pcb check` makes rules from.

**15. `silk-owner` measures the text's anchor point only. By reading only.**
- Where: `src/check.rs:376-379`.
- A left- or right-justified reference has its anchor at one end, so a long designator can lie over a neighbour while its anchor is nearest its own part, and the reverse.

**16. Smaller disagreements. By reading only.**
- `pcb check --help` lists only the 0.4.0 checks.
- `[quality]` values are not validated in `project::check` (a negative or NaN limit is accepted and never fires).
- A `class = "critical"` net that is not on the board is dropped without a word by `route` (`src/route.rs:686`).

### Checked and found correct

- **Via-in-pad rotation and flip:** footprints at 30, 210 and 300 degrees, front and back, against pcbnew's own `HitTest`. The via on the pad was found and the one 1.3 mm across it was not, in all four (`p1.py`).
- **Silk-owner position:** footprints at 0, 30, 135 and 210 degrees, front and back. The reported designator coordinates equal pcbnew's in all five; hidden references are skipped (`p2.py`).
- **Text size order:** `(size h w)` is read as height first.
- **KiCad hole rules:** same-net vias, same-net PTH pads and other-net vias are each found (`p12.py`).
- **Read-back:** `hole_hole_same` at 0.254 and `hole_hole` at 0.5; `silk_on_hole` on a masked-open hole; the mask dam limit follows the mask colour (0.22 mm gap warned on black, not on green).
- **`region_shape`:** a keyhole contour gives 96 of 100 mm², a self-overlapping contour gives 60 mm², and degenerate rings give None.
- **Critical nets:** `critical-open` fires on an open critical net at default severities. With `--rip all`, a hand-laid critical net's segments are unchanged and it is listed in `critical_kept` (`p9.py`).
- **Staged routing:** a run over the cap is rejected, the result goes to `.rejected.kicad_pcb`, and the output is restored byte for byte in place, or removed with its project file when it did not exist before (`p9b.py`).
- **Schemas:** real output of `check`, `check --fab`, `quality` and staged `route` validates against `schemas/*.json`. The `check` enum in `check.json` and `gate.json` matches the findings the code emits, and the waiver list in `project.rs` matches except `spec`, `stale` and `fab`, which is deliberate.
- **`docs/codes.md`:** has `critical-net`, `critical-open`, `via-in-pad`, `silk-on-via` and `mask-dam`.
- **Economic refusals:** read correctly for the 2-, 4- and 6-layer files.
- **Odd input:** no panic on a reference without `at`, an empty `attr`, a via without a drill, or an art group with no or empty `members`, through `check` and the gate (`p11.py`).
