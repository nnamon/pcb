| C1 | SHOULD-FIX | DRC net attribution, `route` still_open | KiCad stores `/` inside a label name as `{slash}` (`EscapeString`, `CTX_NETNAME`), and DRC descriptions show the unescaped name. `item_net` (helper) and `kisexp.drc_counts` look for `[<file name>]`, find nothing, and file the item under `<no net>`. On `k3/names_slash.py`, a clearance between `/TX{slash}RX` and `/SDA{slash}SCL` is keyed `clearance <no net>`, and one between `/TX{slash}RX` and GND is keyed `clearance <no net> GND`. Different real nets merge into one key, so a fixed violation on one can offset a new one on another (the K3 failure, by another route). The badge with `VBUS_F` renamed `VBUS{slash}F` (`k3r/`), `route --nets 'VBUS{slash}F'`: `done`, `still_open: []`, while the after-DRC lists `Track [VBUS/F] … Pad 1 [VBUS/F] of R109` as unconnected. The fixture's plain-name case reports `still_open ["VBUS_F"]`. Labels such as `TX/RX` are common in KiCad schematics, which new projects start from. The oracle copies the helper's matching rule, so it repeats the error and cannot catch it. Fix: resolve each DRC item by the `uuid` the JSON report gives (pcbnew item to net) in the helper, give `kisexp` its own independent method, and add a `/`-label net to the doctor probe and a route case. | Fixed. The helper resolves each DRC item by its uuid in the loaded board (a connected item gives its net; anything else, such as text or a field, none). Items KiCad loads without a stored uuid fall back to the net names, unescaped (`{slash}` and KiCad's other escapes), longest first. The oracles' reader (`kisexp`) maps uuids to nets from the file text instead, with the same fallback written separately. The reviewer's name board is now `tests/lib/names_board.py` with two `{slash}` pairs, checked in `adapter_cases.py` for the helper and for `kisexp`; the 09b95ba helper and reader fail it. The doctor probe net is now `D[0]{slash} x`. |
