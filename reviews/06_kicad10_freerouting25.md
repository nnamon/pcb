# Review 6: KiCad 10.0.8 port and Freerouting 2.5.0 adapter

Independent reviewer, 30 Oct 2026, at commit 2c42179 (scope: 75fe1f2 and 2c42179, `git diff 9ec645d..2c42179 -- tools/pcb`). The reviewer's scripts are in the session scratchpad, `r6/`: `load_compare.py`, `text_vs_pcbnew.py`, `reader_vs_pcbnew.py`, `drc_repeat.py`, `k7/rep7.py`, and the mutation boards under `zm/`, `cm/`, `em/`, `br/`, `dru/`.

**Found sound:**
- `cargo xtask test` with Freerouting 2.5.0 on the host: 20 of 20 steps in 485 s.
- Import writer: the badge import loads in pcbnew 10.0.8 with the same 3255 tracks and vias (net, layer, width, lock, via span), 733 pads and 136 nets as its text says. A pcbnew re-save changes none of these.
- Reader: `pcb info` on a KiCad-saved board (tabs, multi-line, 1140 `(locked yes)` on tracks, vias and footprints) agrees with pcbnew on every pad, track, via and lock of all 136 nets. The only differences are 9 pad coordinates 1 um apart from rounding at the third decimal.
- Lock writer: the route scratch boards (6 and 3242 locked items) load with every lock as written, and the DSN carries them as `(type fix)`.
- KiCad 10 writes copper layers in stack order (`0 F.Cu`, `4 In1.Cu`, `6 In2.Cu`, `2 B.Cu`), the DSN keeps that order, and `--router.layers.routable` is applied by index in it (`ReflectionUtil.setFieldValue`, `RouterSettings.applyBoardSpecificOptimizations`).
- `ignore_other_nets` splits a shared class and copies its `circuit` and `rule`. The badge DSN keeps only USB_SENSE in `kicad_default` and moves the rest into `pcb_not_routed_0` to `_3`. `applyNetClassExclusions` matches the names exactly (case aside), and the autorouter skips those items (`Item.hasIgnoredNets`). The badge reroute reported `dropped_other_nets: 0`.
- Old-format files are refused with `old-kicad-format`; the advised `kicad-cli pcb upgrade` works and the result reads.
- Courtyard allowance in verify_import.py: KiCad 10.0.8 does build U101's courtyard 5 um inside its drawn outline. The check is not weaker: a courtyard moved 3 um out or in fails (`cm/a`, `cm/b`).
- Zone text normalisation in verify_route.py `items()`: it removes only `filled_polygon` children, the fill flag and whitespace runs inside zones. Nothing semantic is hidden.
- DRC through kicad-cli: the same options as before (`--all-track-errors`, zones refilled). `--severity-all` also reports excluded items, which is stricter. The schema check, the `Found N` cross-check and the adapter cases refuse a renamed key or schema.
- The CI image's PPA key fingerprint matches Launchpad's `signing_key_fingerprint` for kicad-10.0-releases; `10.0.8~ubuntu24.04.1` is published there today. Local `pcb-ci:3` holds KiCad 10.0.8, OpenJDK 25.0.4.1, Rust 1.97.0 and jsonschema 4.26.0.

## Not verified

- **CI:** GitHub Actions is unavailable, so the workflows were read, not run. In local `pcb-ci:3`, from a clean archive of 2c42179, `cargo xtask test` passed 20 of 20 steps in 471 s. The doctor step that runs before it failed (K12).
- **Fill counts:** the DESIGN.md claim that KiCad 7.0.11 and 10.0.8 give the same 34 power fills was not re-run.
- **Back-side SMD import:** pcbnew load was checked only through the import cases. The badge has one back-side part (BT101, through-hole).
- **K1 mechanism:** which KiCad 10 change causes it (threaded clearance test or per-item deduplication) was not traced in KiCad source, and no setting that makes it deterministic was found.
