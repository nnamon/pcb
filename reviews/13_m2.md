# Review 13: milestone M2 (zones)

Independent reviewer, 4 Nov 2026. Reviewed `pcb zone` (src/zone.rs, the new pcb.toml fields in src/project.rs, `finish_allowing` and the fill-flag carry-back in src/gate.rs, the `zone` command in src/main.rs) and its tests at commit 128b11f. Saved by the designer from the reviewer's hand-back. The reviewer's scripts joined the suite in `tests/review13/`.

M2 did not pass its gate at review. The fills `pcb zone` wrote were not the fills KiCad makes for the same file (finding 1). After a KiCad refill, the spike 3 oracle failed on the badge. Nothing in the suite checked the badge's stitching vias (finding 2).

**Found sound:**
- **Fill flag.** pcbnew writes `(fill yes` on every zone it fills, and the gate carries the same flag back, hand-made zones included. Without the flag, pcbnew still loads the saved fills (`IsFilled()` false) with the same area, and kicad-cli DRC gives the same counts. So carrying the flag is correct, and `verify_edit.py` reads it only inside zone blocks (fill13).
- **Keep-outs.** The rectangle grows by `grow_mm` with square corners. Layers are `"all"` or the list given. `forbid` maps track, via, footprint and pour to KiCad's flags, and pads are always allowed (feature13 keepout_flags).
- **Zone settings.** pcbnew reads each value as written: clearance, thermal gap and spoke, priority, minimum width, `connect` (solid 2, none 0, thermal 1), and island removal "always" (feature13 zone_settings).
- **Fill overlaps.** Where two fills overlap, the one with the higher `fill_priority` keeps the copper (7.8 mm² against 0.02 mm²). No fill copper lies outside the board (feature13 fill_priority).
- **Net-tie geometry.** Tie footprints turned 0°, 30° and 90° on top, and 0° and 135° on the bottom: tie copper plus retyped tracks match the original tracks exactly (ends, widths, layers), and the via pads sit where the vias were. A second run writes the same bytes (tie13 turn_*). Vias become pads in x then y order (tie13 vias_only).
- **Stitching margins.** On a busy board at a 1.5 mm pitch, vias keep at least 0.606 mm from other-net tracks, 0.675 mm from other-net vias, 0.864 mm from an NPTH hole, 1.45 mm from the edge and 0.828 mm from same-net pads (thermal connect). Vias are 0.9 mm apart (stitch13 margins).
- **KiCad re-save.** After pcbnew re-saves the zoned board, the next run removes its own 2 zones and all 215 vias, then writes 215 again with no duplicates (stitch13 resave).
- **Changed settings.** On a zoned board, dropped zones and a dropped keep-out are removed (5 zones). A hand-made zone and via are kept with their text unchanged (feature13 changed). The suite covers a stitching via moved by hand.
- **Refusals.** Each of these is refused with the right code, nothing written and the input untouched: unknown key, NaN, zero minimum width, bad `connect`, missing fill or keep-out layer, bad `layers` word, unknown stitch net, drill not below size, negative `max`, `[nets.X]` for a missing net, pad override without a dot, schema 2, no pcb.toml, missing board, `-o` a folder, `-o` in a missing folder, `--work` a file, `-o` on the gate's candidate file (`output-in-work`). A rejected result aimed at the input goes to `board.rejected.kicad_pcb` and the input stays (refuse13).
- **Output contract.** Every one of the 70-odd runs in the probes keeps it (`contract_check.py`).
- **DRC allowance.** Growth exactly at `max` (1, and 2) is accepted and warned `drc-allowed`. Growth above `max` is rejected, and so is growth on other nets or under a misspelt kind. With an allowed growth and a real regression together, the run is rejected and lists both. An allowance on a kind that did not grow changes nothing (refuse13 allow_*).
- **Badge result.** The run is done and DRC goes from 144 to 27. It makes 2 keep-outs, 4 zones and 34 fills. The tie gets 49 copper segments, 2 retyped tracks and via pads 3 and 4. It places 202 GND stitching vias: none on a pad, none within 0.3 mm of another net's pad, none in a keep-out. verify_zones passes on the file as written (badge13).
- **Badge allowances.** The two allowances cover exactly what their `why` says. `starved_thermal GND`: J102 pad 4 has 1 of 2 spokes on F.Cu, and J701 pad 2 has spokes into an isolated island. `clearance <no net> NFC_AC1`: two L601 coil segments sit 0.075 mm from L601 pad 3.
- **J102 solid override.** A solid override on J102 pad 4 trips check D on In1.Cu and In2.Cu, and the starved count drops to 1 (badge13 badge_j102_solid).
