# Review 15: milestone M3 (check and release)

Independent reviewer, 4 Nov 2026, on branch `pcb-m3-import` at commit db059a1 (diff against M2 head dc10ccd): `check`, `gate`, `release`, `compare-gerbers`, `paste`, `art`, the import additions, and the paddle-keyer golden test. Probes in `tests/review15/`, verdicts in `tests/review15/check.py`.

The M3 gate is not met as the test stands. The golden comparison passes a release with whole layers missing (1) and with every 0.22 mm top-layer power track narrowed to 0.15 mm (3), and the released Gerbers and CPL use different origins, 50.85 mm apart, which the comparison's alignment hides (4).

## Found sound

- **Inputs of the golden test.** `golden/` is byte-identical to `paddle-keyer/order/` (three SHA-256 matches). `circuit.json` and four of the five art files are byte-identical to `paddle-keyer/design/` (`final.circuit.json`, `art/*.json`). The Gerber comparison takes nothing from the ordered output. The CPL comparison does for J1 and J2 (27).
- **Golden sensitivity, faults built on the board, refilled by `pcb zone`, exported as release exports.** Each of these is rejected: a 0.15 mm XOUT track removed on F.Cu and a TIP track removed on B.Cu; R11 moved 0.2 mm; a TIP via removed; a no-net copper square; U1's silkscreen outline removed; J1's slot made non-plated; the `v1.0.0` label moved 0.3 mm or 0.08 mm, its text changed to `v1.0.1`, its cap height to 0.9 mm; a slot 0.2 mm longer. Probes `golden15`, `compare15`.
- **P1, P2.** Release with no gate is refused `gate-missing` and makes no folder. Changing R11's value after the gate makes it stale, and release is refused `gate-stale`. Probe `release15` `p1_no_gate`, `p2_value_changed`.
- **Fab rule sets.** With the board's own rules opened to zero, each 2-layer rule fires on its own: a 0.12 mm track, a 0.12 mm gap, a 0.25/0.5 mm via, holes of two nets 0.4 mm apart, copper 0.2 mm from the edge. P35: on 4 layers, an In1 track 0.15 mm from a via hole and one 0.25 mm from a 1.0 mm component hole each give `hole_clearance`; an In1 track 0.3 mm from a via hole gives nothing. Probe `check15` `fab2`, `fab4`.
- **P10.** XOUT at 2 A against its 0.15 mm F.Cu track: IPC-2221 asks 0.781 mm, finding raised. The inner-layer figure (0.5 oz) checks by hand too. Probe `check15` `p10_width`.
- **CPL places.** Offsets on J1 turned to 30°, on J2 flipped (0° to 180°) and on C2 flipped and turned (150°), each with a two-component offset, land where pcbnew's own footprint transform puts the offset point, within 1 µm. DNP, excluded from BOM, excluded from position files and hand-fit parts are each left out of both files. Probe `release15` `cpl_offsets`, `dnp_handfit`.
- **Reproducibility.** Two releases give byte-identical BOM and CPL; the zips hold the same names and differ only in KiCad's creation-date lines; `CHECKSUMS.txt` matches every file. Probe `release15` `repro`.
- **Read-back on real boards.** A GND track over a VBUS pad is rejected as `short: GND / VBUS`. On the badge (4 layers) it finds the three known opens (GND at U501 pad 21, PMUX_ILM, VBUS_F), no shorts and no unplaced pad, and the LED_A4 via's 0.022 mm gap to BAT_RAW on all four layers. Probes `release15` `rb_short_folder`, `badge15`.
- **Badge through `pcb check`.** Every item DESIGN.md lists is found with its count (PMUX_ILM, VBUS_F, LED_A4 4 clearance and 8 hole clearance, U501 GND, a dangling GND via, three starved thermals, NFC_AC1 clearance, 7 courtyard overlaps). Probe `badge15` `badge_check`.
- **Paste.** A second run writes the same bytes. Dropping every rule puts U1 and J1 back to the text the import wrote. Three 0.9 mm windows on U1 turned 30° all fall inside pad 57's copper. Probe `pa15`.
- **Art and text, read from kicad-cli's Gerbers.** A plate with three staggered holes, a C shape with a hole, and a ring with an island inside plot with 0 mm² symmetric difference from the GeoJSON. `F8R` on the back is the exact mirror of the front (0 mm²), centred on `at`, with the three counters of `8` and `R` open. An `H` at cap 1.5 mm, grown 0.05 mm and turned 90°, is 1.6 mm across. A second run writes the same bytes; dropped entries leave no group. Probe `pa15` `art`, `art_dropped`.
- **Import silkscreen.** All 603 footprint silkscreen segments on turned badge parts sit where the circuit JSON puts them. A bottom part turned 90° with bottom silkscreen (no fixture has one) lands on B.SilkS at the right place, pads included. Probe `import15` `badge_silk`, `bottom_silk`.
- **compare-gerbers inputs.** A zip and a folder of the same files compare equal; layers named only by X2 are all found. Probe `compare15`.
