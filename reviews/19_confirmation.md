# Review 19: confirmation of reviews 17 and 18 (milestones M3 and M4)

Independent reviewer, 5 Nov 2026, worktree at commit fabb4aa, binary built from that tree. Probes in `tests/review19/`, verdicts in `tests/review19/check.py`: 65 verdicts pass, 5 fail (47 three times, 48, 49), 24 notes. Exit 1. Review 17's runner passes 41 of 41 on this binary, and review 18's 32 of 32.

Of the 14 closures, 13 hold for what they claim and one is rejected on documentation only (42). Three new findings could put a wrong part, drill or rotation on an ordered board:
- A slot whose centre is off its pad's centre in JLCPCB's footprint is drilled at the pad's centre (47).
- A polarised two-pad part matched by shape still passes turned 180° (48).
- `pcb sync` drops the schematic's DNP and "exclude from BOM" flags, so release orders those parts (49).

| 47 | FINDING | A slot whose centre is off its pad's centre is drilled at the pad's centre. `src/easyeda.rs` reads only the hole points' direction and writes `(drill oval w h)` with no offset. Evidence: a 14 × 6 unit pad, slot hole points 2 units (0.508 mm) along the slot from the pad centre: sync done, both drill ends 0.508 mm from EasyEDA's hole points, upright and turned 90°. | 48 | FINDING | Closure 5 left the modulo-180 match for every two-pad part matched by shape, polarised or not. ## Rerunning the probes

```
cd tools/pcb && cargo build --release
PCB_FREEROUTING_JAR=/opt/freerouting/freerouting-2.5.0.jar python3 tests/review19/check.py target/release/pcb /tmp/review19/run
PCB_R19_BASE=<a project the M4 flow has released> python3 tests/review19/check.py target/release/pcb /tmp/review19/run pins19.py
```

Without `PCB_R19_BASE` the first probe that needs it runs the M4 fixture flow into `workdir/_flow` (a few minutes, mostly the route). Probes: `stale19.py` (closures 39, 41 and 44 on the M4 board: harmless saves, paste and art output changed, netlist and place changes), `fab19.py` (closure 40 on pcbnew-built boards, gaps measured in the released Gerbers), `pins19.py` (closures 1 and 2, the pins18.py edit), `slot19.py` (closure 3 against pcbnew's own drill shapes, and J1), `twopad19.py` (closure 5, `economic-unknown`), `resync19.py` (closure 4), `dnp19.py` (49), `oracle19.py` (closure 6, mutations of the M4 release run through `verify_against_v1.py`). Shared helpers in `r19lib.py`, which uses `tests/lib` only for the output contract.
